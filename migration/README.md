# ROWAD Platform Consolidation

This workspace consolidates the Accounting and Business Administration platforms under one application.

## Source preservation rule
- Accounting source is the technical foundation for the unified platform.
- Business Administration source is mounted under `src/domains/business` without deleting or rewriting its educational content.
- Domain-specific functionality remains isolated until the corresponding consolidation phase is verified.

## Current status
- M0 — Forensic baseline: in progress / artifacts generated alongside this project.
- Initial domain mount: implemented.
- Shared identity, curriculum, assessment and analytics consolidation: NOT YET CLAIMED COMPLETE.

No completion claim should be made for later phases until their verification gates pass.
